How the numbers are made

A win-probability model, built from the supplied ball-by-ball data and scored out-of-fold, estimates each side's chance of winning before every ball. Leverage is how far the possible outcomes of a ball could move that chance; pressure balls are the top 20% by leverage within each innings, after dropping the 10% of balls in already-decided games.

Each ball credits the batter or bowler with the win probability he added, divided by its leverage. A penalised regression with opponent, innings, phase and format effects gives each player a usual level and a pressure lift; rating = level + lift. Ranks use the 20th percentile over 2,000 match-bootstrap refits. Batting ranks 7–15 and bowling ranks 9–11 are statistically tied.

An independent blind audit rebuilt the analysis without seeing these rankings; its fixes (a data leak, more resamples, fairer eligibility) are applied here. Lift is shown as past pattern only: it does not repeat between halves of the data.
